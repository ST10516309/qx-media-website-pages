About QX Media

Our Story

QX Media(Pty)Ltd was founded in 2023 by Siphile Mbanga and Mayibongwe Lucky Mhlongo as a small freelance social media consultancy, initially assisting businesses with content creation and community engagement. Within two years the agency expanded into paid social media advertising, analytics-led strategy, and community management retainers, building a small in-house creative team to serve a broader base of small businesses, e-commerce brands, and corporate clients as a full digital marketing agency.

Mission & Vision

Vision: To become the digital marketing partner South African businesses trust first for creative, data-informed growth.

Mission: To help brands stop guessing and start growing, by building strategies rooted in audience behaviour and measurable campaign results.
